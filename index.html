Design Principles

How I Build AI & Operational Systems

01 · AUDIT FIRST

Map Before Automating

Shadow frontline workflows to isolate broken inputs and exception paths—never automate bad data faster.

02 · DETERMINISTIC CORE

Zero Hallucinated Math

Keep reconciliation, keys, and quotas strictly in SQL, Python, Power Query, and Pydantic schemas; reserve AI for unstructured edges.

03 · HUMAN GATES

Safe Escalation

Auto-mute chatbots when reps intervene (is_echo/fromMe) and gate irreversible worker actions behind explicit approvals.

04 · ADOPTION

Ship for Non-Tech Teams

Expose clean Chatwoot inboxes, Teams cards, and self-serve Star Schemas with SOPs teams actually trust.
